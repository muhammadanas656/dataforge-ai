# DataForge AI — Legal, Intellectual Property & Design Standards Guide

## 1. Intellectual Property & Design Ownership

### What You Own:
- **Generated Vector Assets:** You own full commercial rights to the specific SVG markup, React JSX, and Vue 3 source code synthesized by DataForge AI.
- **Derived Customizations:** Any variations, palette alterations, and component modifications integrated into your web applications belong exclusively to your organization.

### Public Domain & Standards Foundations:
- **TRIZ Inventive Operators:** Genrich Altshuller's 40 Inventive Principles are in the public domain.
- **Design Tokens Community Group (DTCG):** Standardized design token schemas follow the open W3C DTCG format.
- **WCAG 2.1 AAA:** Accessibility compliance adheres to the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) Web Accessibility Initiative guidelines.

---

## 2. Trademark Risk Mitigation & Brand Safety

- **Zero Direct Trademark Replication:** DataForge AI synthesizes original geometric combinations and never reproduces proprietary brand logos (e.g. Apple, Google, Microsoft, Nike).
- **Customization Recommended:** We recommend configuring design tokens (primary colors, border radiuses, typography) to match your organizational brand identity.
- **High-Stakes Verification:** For international trademark filings, conduct a standard USPTO / EUIPO trademark clearance search.

---

## 3. Accessibility & Compliance Guarantees

- **WCAG 2.1 Level AAA:** Enforces high contrast ratios ($\ge 7.0:1$), accessible naming (`<title>`, `aria-label`), and `role="img"` semantics.
- **Multi-Framework Integrity:** Transpiled React JSX and Vue 3 components preserve semantic DOM structure for screen readers and keyboard navigation.
